Greenhouse handbook

How to keep things mostly alive

1

Read the morning forecast and choose a nursery lot you can afford.

2

Open its cartons, then water, mist, or prune plants that need attention.

3

Arrange the prepared plants on your displays, then open the shop when you are ready.

4

Read Must have, Would love, and Budget; retag a plant when the price is not right.

Keyboard: Q cycles shop objects, E acts, 1/2/3 use care tools, 4/5/6 choose Quick/Fair/Boutique, and arrow keys turn the view.

Plants droop, but never die. There are no impatient customers. Explore at your own pace.
