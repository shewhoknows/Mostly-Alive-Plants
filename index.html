Greenhouse handbook

How to keep things mostly alive

1

Visitor demand changes each day. Check the market strip before you buy stock.

2

Unsold plants stay in the shop. Keep a useful range, but plan for daily costs. From Week 4, stock above the target uses 2 extra coins per plant at closing.

3

Read Must have, Would love, and Budget. Care, arrange, or retag a plant to improve the match.

4

Select the 3D Care Bench to open its jobs. Rehabilitate clears nursery stress and restores rescue value. Normal care does not. The grow lamp improves new bench jobs.

5

Open the Retail Supply Shelf to buy clip lights, fertilizer, and treatments. Keep fertilizer, fungicide, and potting soil ready for customer add-ons.

6

From Week 2, accept one optional order. Hold healthy matching plants, then complete the order by Friday. Neighborhood events also change one type of demand.

Keyboard: Q cycles shop objects, E acts, 1/2/3 use care tools, 4/5/6 choose Quick/Fair/Boutique, and arrow keys turn the view.

Plants droop, but never die. There are no impatient customers. Explore at your own pace.
